## 🧭 Overview

Institutional maintenance management becomes increasingly challenging as organizations expand their physical infrastructure. Paper-based and fragmented manual processes can contribute to inefficient request handling, delayed repairs, communication gaps, and increased operational costs.

This research examines digital maintenance-tracking approaches for environments such as universities, hospitals, and corporate campuses. It discusses how cloud computing, workflow automation, data analytics, mobile accessibility, real-time notifications, and predictive-maintenance capabilities can support the maintenance lifecycle—from request submission through task completion and feedback.

## 📝 Abstract

The paper explores the role of a centralized digital maintenance-tracking approach in addressing operational challenges within institutions. It considers existing maintenance-management practices, system architecture, and implementation methodology, while examining user adoption, integration, scalability, usability, and organizational change.

The work focuses on how structured digital workflows can improve visibility and coordination among requesters, technicians, supervisors, and administrators. The repository contains the published paper and its official publication documents.

## 🎯 Research problem

Maintenance requests are often distributed across phone calls, emails, paper forms, and informal conversations. This fragmentation can make requests difficult to track, create communication gaps, reduce visibility into work progress, and complicate reporting and accountability.

The research examines how a centralized maintenance-tracking platform can help address these process challenges.

## 🔍 Research objectives

1. Examine institutional maintenance-management approaches and identify their limitations.
2. Study an architecture for a digital maintenance-tracking platform.
3. Consider the requirements of different institutional user groups.
4. Explore workflow automation, mobile access, cloud infrastructure, and integration capabilities.
5. Discuss implementation, user adoption, and continuous-improvement considerations.
